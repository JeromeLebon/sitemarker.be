How to Create Your Site Plan in Minutes

SiteMarker is designed to be used immediately, without any training or software installation.

1

Search your location

Type your worksite address in the search bar. SiteMarker will navigate to the exact location on OpenStreetMap.

2

Add your markers

Drag road signs, safety cones, and equipment icons from the toolbar directly onto the map at the correct positions.

3

Draw zones & routes

Use the brush tool to define work areas, detour routes, or pedestrian zones. Adjust colors to distinguish different areas.

4

Export your plan

Download the map as a PNG for immediate use in documents, emails, or permit applications. Save as JSON to edit later.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SiteMarker completely free to use?

Yes, SiteMarker is entirely free. There are no subscription fees, no account registration, and no limitations on how many plans you can create or export. The tool is funded by non-intrusive advertisements.

Do I need to create an account to save my work?

No account is required. Your work is saved locally as a JSON project file that you download to your computer. You can reopen this file at any time to continue editing your site plan.

Can I use SiteMarker on a mobile phone or tablet?

Yes, SiteMarker is fully responsive and works on smartphones and tablets. The mobile interface includes a sidebar menu for accessing all tools. For complex plans with many markers, a desktop or laptop is recommended for the best experience.

What map data does SiteMarker use?

SiteMarker uses OpenStreetMap (OSM), a free and open global map maintained by a community of volunteers. The map data is highly accurate for most areas and is updated continuously. Satellite imagery is not currently included.

Can I add my own custom icons or company logos?

Yes. SiteMarker supports custom SVG file uploads. You can add your own signage icons, company logos, or industry-specific markers to the toolbar, where they will persist between sessions using your browser's local storage.

Is the exported PNG suitable for official permit applications?

SiteMarker exports high-resolution PNG images that are suitable for inclusion in documents, PDFs, and formal applications. However, compliance with local authority requirements varies by region — always verify the specific format requirements with the relevant authority before submission.

What browsers are supported?

SiteMarker works on all modern browsers including Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, and Safari. For the best performance, we recommend using an up-to-date version of Chrome or Firefox.
